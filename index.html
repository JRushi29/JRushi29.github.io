    Water resources researcher, building decision support

    Turning models of water into decisions people can use.

    I'm Rushikesh, a remote sensing & GIS researcher and agricultural engineer. I build hydrological and hydraulic models, automate the workflows around them, and wrap the results in dashboards that researchers, administrators and engineers can act on. This site is the running log of that work — and of everything else.
