About

Hi, I'm Rushikesh Jadhav.

I'm a researcher in remote sensing and GIS, specialising in water resources. I trained as an agricultural engineer and completed my M.Tech in Remote Sensing & GIS at the Indian Institute of Remote Sensing (IIRS).

Today my work is hydrological and hydraulic modelling — HEC-HMS, HEC-RAS and SWAT — driven by satellite and weather-forecast data. I automate the workflows around these models in Python and build dashboards and decision-support tools for researchers, government administrators and private companies.

The long-term goal: to be useful at the point where water science meets decisions.

This log is where that work meets everything else — trips, books and opinions included.
