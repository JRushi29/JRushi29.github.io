    The Water, Climate & AI Brief

    What happened this week in AI and machine learning for hydrology, floods, groundwater, climate and agriculture, and why it matters.

    Edition 001First issue coming soon5-minute read

    A short Saturday read on where water science and AI are heading.

    Each edition picks the week's most useful developments and says in one line why each one matters.

    It covers new models and open tools for flood and hydrology work, new satellite missions and datasets, government programmes in water and agriculture, and one research gap worth working on. Every item links to its source.

    Who it's for: researchers, engineers and administrators who need to keep up without reading everything.
